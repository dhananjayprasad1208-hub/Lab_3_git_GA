The dusty neon sign outside the alleyway diner flickered, casting long, rhythmic shadows across the pavement. Leo adjusted his leather jacket against the cool night air, eyeing the quiet street corner. It was past midnight, the exact time the rumors said the midnight courier would show up.

He slid into a worn vinyl booth near the back of the diner. The scent of stale coffee and rain hung heavy in the air. The bell above the door chimed, and a tall figure in a dark trench coat walked in, holding a plain silver briefcase. Without saying a word, the stranger sat directly across from Leo.

"You brought the drive?" the stranger asked, his voice low and raspy.

Leo reached into his pocket and pulled out a small, metallic flash drive. "Everything is on here. Every blueprint, every access code. But you promised answers."

The stranger tapped the briefcase. "The answers you want aren't easily digested, kid. Once you know who was really running the operation, there's no going back to a normal life."

Leo hesitated, his fingers tightening around the drive. He had spent months chasing down clues, dodging security patrols, and risking everything to uncover the truth about his father's sudden disappearance. Turning back now wasn't an option.

He slid the drive across the Formica table. The stranger picked it up, inspected it briefly, and pressed a sequence of numbers into the briefcase lock. It clicked open, revealing a thick leather-bound journal and a single brass key.

"Your father left this for you," the stranger said, sliding the journal over. "He knew you'd come looking."
